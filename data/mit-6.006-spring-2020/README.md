# Original lecture corpus

These five unchanged lecture PDFs are from MIT OpenCourseWare, **6.006
Introduction to Algorithms, Spring 2020**, taught by Erik Demaine, Jason Ku and
Justin Solomon. The original attribution sheets remain attached to every PDF.

[Official lecture index](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-spring-2020/pages/lecture-notes/)
and [MIT OCW terms](https://ocw.mit.edu/terms/).

`corpus.json` records the exact original URLs, SHA-256 checksums, original PDF page
numbers and teaching/attribution page lists. There are 29 original pages: 24
teaching pages and five final attribution sheets. Lectures 2 and 3 are development
material. Lectures 4, 6 and 8 are the held-out final chapter corpus.

Only these PDF files are uploaded/indexed as course evidence. The separate
`evaluation_assets` folder contains assistant-authored question drafts and
annotation forms; none of those files belongs in the retrieval corpus.
